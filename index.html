K4 • Group A Slides 12–14

Wiener Process (Brownian Motion)

Models pure continuous random noise steps where each step change is defined as:

Δz = ε √Δt  |  ε ~ N(0,1)
  • Mean step: E[Δz] = 0
  • Variance: Var[Δz] = Δt
  • Time Scaling: Var[z(T) - z(0)] = T
K6 • Group B Slides 22–29

Itô Process & GBM

Adapts continuous noise to stock price movements where return percentage is normally distributed:

dS = μS dt + σS dz
  • Drift component: Expected growth μS dt
  • Stochastic component: Random noise σS dz
  • Distribution: ΔS/S ~ φ(μΔt, σ²Δt)
K8 • Group B Slides 33–34

Parameters μ and σ

Distinguishes expected market return from volatility and derivative valuation dynamics:

SD(S_T) = σ √T
  • Expected return μ: Physical asset trend
  • Volatility σ: Drives option pricing & risk
  • Risk-Neutrality: Derivative pricing drops μ
